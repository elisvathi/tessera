The ten changes that matter most

Ranked by what they give a producer at a desk and a performer on a stage, for the effort. Phases are in §6 of the proposal.

  1. 1
    A first minute that just works
    A loop dropped on the panel plays when you press PLAY. With the editor closed, the display says what happened. When a synth already uses the Audio module's inputs, one helper puts Tessera's mixer in front of it.
    A1 · L4 · P5EveryoneS–M · Phase 1
  2. 2
    A mixer that fits any height, and scrolls
    Strips, compact strips, tiles or meters, picked by height; strips scroll under a pinned Master; a level bar in every track row; keys in the mixer.
    M1 · L2 · M3Everyone who mixesM · Phase 1
  3. 3
    Audio slots that loop like a looper
    ● records into an empty slot, a second press closes the loop on the beat, ● on the playing loop overdubs a layer, undo peels it back. REC and Perform drive the same steps.
    S1PerformersL · Phase 2
  4. 4
    The Perform module
    Eight strips of launch, stop, send, level, mute, solo and arm, all Rack parameters for MIDI-Map, with poly CV inputs and an EOC jack per strip.
    P3PerformersL · Phase 3
  5. 5
    Keep every pass, comp by swiping
    Loop passes on audio tracks always become takes; takes show as rows; drag across one to use it there; an IN button takes the mic out of the mix.
    R1 · R2 · R4ProducersM · Phase 2
  6. 6
    One view across both editors
    Lanes, folds, Notes or Mixer, drum view and the lower lane follow you between the panel editor and the full editor; size, zoom and scroll stay per editor.
    L1EveryoneS–M · Phase 1
  7. 7
    Automate every mixer control
    Group, drum-lane and source levels; Mute and Record switch lanes, also for another track; a Tempo lane on the Master row.
    M4 · S6Producers, loopersM–L · Phase 4
  8. 8
    An audio editor you can work in
    The clip's settings in its header, Q pins hits to the grid with a strength after showing the drift, a Slice tool, fades, preview while stopped.
    A2–A5ProducersM · Phase 2
  9. 9
    Safe on stage
    Bars-left counts and record buttons in the perform view, Lock editing, Stop now, LAUNCH that walks the set, sections with Next song, Perform keys anywhere in Rack.
    S2–S5 · P2PerformersM · Phase 3
  10. 10
    Modules that say more
    EOC beside AUTO on Track Out and on Drum Out, each output module's screen shows mute, solo, arm and what plays, and an FX Loop module gives the agreed effect chain a home.
    P1 · P4Rack patchersS, L · Phases 1, 4

The rack

What a performer can reach without the editor. Today the rack holds the transport and the scenes; no track can be mapped, patched or watched. The proposal adds no jack to Tessera and no HP to Track Out: an EOC jack takes the free half of AUTO's row, and two new modules carry the controls.

The editor inside the panel

Tessera widened to 80 HP: the panel editor is 799 × 348 px at 100% rack zoom, and its lower pane gets 70–200 px. That is where today's mixer gives up. The proposal keeps the same pane and the same F4 key (DECISIONS §118) and changes what a short pane shows.

The audio editor

Making a beat from a loose break. Today the audio editor shows the file and the clip's window; its settings live in menus, and warping is one hit at a time or every hit to 1/16. The proposal puts the clip in the header, shows the drift before you fix it, and makes quantizing audio the same key as quantizing notes.

Journey 3 · R1 R2 R3 R4 R5

Recording and comping

Four passes of a vocal over a four-bar loop. Today, in the default record mode, each pass replaces the last on the timeline; in Stack mode the passes are kept, and comping is a right-click per part. The proposal keeps every pass on an audio track, and comps by dragging across the take you want, on the model that exists today (split plus a take per part, DECISIONS §129).

Live looping · S1

Loops in slots

Today an audio take recorded while the clips play lands in the first empty slot as a clip that doesn't loop and isn't launched (TesseraCapture.cpp:723). Notes already loop and overdub. The proposal gives audio the same steps, with a ● on the slot, REC on the panel, or ▶ on the Perform module, so a foot switch through MIDI-Map can run it.

Keys · checked against Shortcuts.cpp and EDITOR_UX §7

Keys

Every binding goes in the one action table, so the help overlay and the tooltips list it. One rule changes: a binding scoped to the pane under the mouse wins over an Everywhere one, so the mixer and the audio editor can give the arrows their own meaning. Today no Everywhere chord collides with a scoped one, so nothing that works now changes.

For the user · answer by number

Open questions

Each has a recommendation. Questions 2, 3, 4, 7 and 16 touch choices already recorded (DECISIONS §17, §77 with §119, §119, the open half of AUDIO_MIXER.md §12, §128).

  1. 1The mixer when the pane is short
    • (a) Compact strips, then tiles, then meters
    • (b) Under 150 px, hide it; use the rows' level bars
    • (c) Keep full strips and scroll up and down
    Rec. (a): short panes keep pan, M, S and a usable fader.
  2. 2One view across both editors (against DECISIONS §17)
    • (a) Share what you look at; sizes per editor
    • (b) Share everything, zoom and scroll too
    • (c) Keep separate; add "Match the other editor"
    Rec. (a): answers the Trello card; the panel keeps its small sizes.
  3. 3Loop passes on audio tracks (against §77, §119)
    • (a) Always takes on audio; Merge/Stack/Replace stay for notes
    • (b) One setting for both, Stack the new default
    • (c) As today
    Rec. (a): losing passes silently is the worst a recorder can meet.
  4. 4Audio loops in slots (against §119)
    • (a) Loop like notes, and overdub: each pass mixed with the loop into a new take; undo peels layers
    • (b) Loop like notes, no overdub: each pass a take, comped later
    • (c) As today
    Rec. (a): it is what live looping is, and keeps "never layer".
  5. 5Hardware moves and undo
    • (a) One undo step once the control rests a second
    • (b) Not undoable, like Rack's mapped knobs
    • (c) Only mouse drags on the module
    Rec. (a): every edit stays undoable without flooding the list.
  6. 6The Perform module's shape
    • (a) One 30 HP module, eight strips, banks
    • (b) A 4 HP strip per track, by each voice
    • (c) Both, (b) later
    Rec. (a): eight sliders map once; banks reach 64 tracks.
  7. 7The effect chain's slot (AUDIO_MIXER.md §12)
    • (a) A 5 HP FX Loop module: SEND, RETURN, WET, BYPASS
    • (b) A Track Out in an "effect slot" role
    • (c) Not now: patch effects in series between OUT and IN
    Rec. (a): the panel says what it does; its controls map.
  8. 8What "global keyboard shortcuts" means
    • (a) Opt-in Perform keys anywhere in Rack, editor closed
    • (b) Editor keys that ignore which pane the mouse is over
    • (c) Keys you can remap (EDITOR_UX §7's v1.1 item)
    Rec. (a) now, (c) later: (b) breaks the scoped keys.
  9. 9A set of songs
    • (a) Sections in one song, Next song, scene follow
    • (b) CV picks an output module's Tessera and track, at a launch line, gates dropped first, "CV" on its screen
    • (c) A set list of project files, preloaded and swapped
    Rec. (a), then (b) for separate patches per song.
  10. 10Metronome when an audio track is first armed
    • (a) Switch to Count-in only, and say so
    • (b) Leave it Off
    • (c) Ask once
    Rec. (a).
  11. 11Switch lanes
    • (a) Under their track, with "Switches: another track"
    • (b) Only their own track
    • (c) Only on the Master row, aimed at any track
    Rec. (a): lanes stay where people look.
  12. 12Slice to a drum track (Tessera plays slices from drum lanes)
    • (a) Build it, in Phase 4
    • (b) No: timeline chops and Make triggers for rack samplers
    Rec. (a): the classic way to make a beat from a break.
  13. 13EOC per track
    • (a) A jack beside AUTO, on Drum Out's poly row, and per Perform strip
    • (b) A ninth channel on the AUTO poly jack
    • (c) Only on Perform
    Rec. (a).
  14. 14What Lock editing locks
    • (a) The song's content; performing and mixing stay live
    • (b) Mixer and tempo too: only launching stays
    • (c) No lock; undo is enough
    Rec. (a).
  15. 15The first loop on the timeline
    • (a) Set the song loop around it and turn it on
    • (b) Lengthen the clip to 8 bars
    • (c) As today
    Rec. (a): it cycles at once; one undo takes it back.
  16. 16Warp markers and the clip's window (against §128)
    • (a) Window and edges drag with markers, which move with the window; tempo drag stays off
    • (b) As built: all locked until the markers go
    Rec. (a): trimming a warped loop shouldn't lose its warp.
